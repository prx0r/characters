  from inside, gene-pool interference for resources/territory.
- **Orion Empire**: reported alignment hub for renegade Pleiadians and Grey
  groups; Angelic-Corps psychic monitoring claimed in response.
- **Galacterian Alignment** (almanac appendix, unpulled): alignment chart of
  space peoples and planets — pull before citing specifics.
- **Grey castes**: short Greys described as degraded by inbreeding/cloning;
  extinction-driven abductions to upgrade; metallic implants in abductees.
