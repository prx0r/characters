## Paraphrased points
- Genesis: 1943 letter from the obscure Shaver to Amazing reproducing a supposed ancient Lemurian alphabet. Published Jan 1944. Correspondence with editor Ray Palmer follows.
- 10,000-word manuscript "A Warning to Future Man" rewritten by Palmer as the novella "I Remember Lemuria!" (Mar 1945, Shaver by-line), presented as true story via racial memory. Shaver's own claim-shape: knowledge received directly from tunnel/cave-network beings.
- Lemuria = 19th-century invention, sunk ~12,000 years ago (file flags this explicitly).
- Response: flood of reader letters, some reporting corroborating experiences. Palmer promotes for three decades; Shaver tells the stories till death.
