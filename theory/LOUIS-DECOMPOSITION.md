## The theory (why the mix works)

1. **Confession-booth engine.** Sincere frame (claims + 1.67s pauses +
   low dynamics 19.6dB = intimacy) → absurd content literalized →
   escalation → SELF-payment. The audience is in a booth, not a theater.
2. **Pauses are load-bearing twice** (dpv): they make sincerity
   believable AND scaffold the surprise window (prediction generation).
   His1.67s > pros'1.39s is why his "nothing" moments feel earned.
3. **Self-directed violation** = the safety valve. Superioirty Theory
   inverted: he's superior to HIMSELF — audiences laugh without cruelty.
4. **Forward-spiral structure** (escalate, never callback): each set is
   a one-way confession ladder. Variety comes from the confession TOPIC,
   not the structure — hence per-clip dominant signatures below.
