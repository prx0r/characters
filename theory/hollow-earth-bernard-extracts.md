## Extract 3 — flight log sample (0600-0910 HOURS entries; note engine trouble + yellowish/reddish ice patterning + circling)

> "0910 HOURS- Vast Ice and snow below, note coloration of yellowish nature, and dispersed in a linear pattern. Altering course for a better examination of this color pattern below, note reddish or purple color also. Circle this area two full turns"
