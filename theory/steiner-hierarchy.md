## Paraphrased structure (verify line-refs before stage use)
- Saturn recapitulation: Thrones/Will lay the body ground; Seraphim/Love, Cherubim/Harmony, Kyriotetes, Dynamis work across periods; human phantoms flicker with borrowed life, ever-changing forms.

- Night-shift doctrine: Sleep/Death + Life-after-Death chapters = the soul's second office.
