## The seven mechanisms

1. **Premise clarity first.** Two-part premise in two sentences: (a) playing
   Monopoly with daughters, (b) she's learning what it means to lose. Chris
   Rock rule: if the audience doesn't understand the premise, nothing works.
   OUR MAP: identity-establish beats (dpv scaffold) — non-negotiable.

2. **Micro-surprises INSIDE the premise.** "not emotionally developed enough
   to handle losing" → "to handle her": two small comedic surprises, each
   with its own PAUSE so each lands in its own beat. OUR MAP: dpv coupling
   at word-granularity, not just beat granularity. Surprise units nest.

3. **Counterpoint as collision engine.** Candyland (visual innocence:
   "fudgy thing", "gummy twirly-o's") vs Monopoly (ruthless property
   seizure). The game choice is "pulled out of thin air" but perfectly
   opposes the premise. OUR MAP: WORLD-MAPPING collision shapes —
   the counterpoint IS the semantic distance. Specific sensory words
   carry it (not abstract labels).

4. **Anticipatory laughter.** After premise+counterpoint are vividly set,
   laughs start BEFORE the punchline — the audience imagines the dark
   turn. "He gradually increases these anticipatory laughs, like the
   audience is a fiddle." OUR MAP: independently confirmed — 78% of
   Casey-corpus laughs start before the riff ends. The engine must
   schedule SETUP VIVIDNESS to harvest anticipation laughs, not just
   the punchline button.

5. **Dramatization locks perspective.** Act-out + hand movement ON the
   punchline "locks the audience into the space" — six-year-old POV makes
   the reality darker (property seizure through her eyes). OUR MAP:
   physicality axis; act-outs = attention pins. Audio proxy: the
   deadpan/dramatic voice shift.

6. **Stay in the bit (Seinfeld rule).** During laughs: emphasize the last
   line, add NO new information, ride the same energy. "Telling a joke is
   like riding a wave of feeling." OUR MAP: overlap-style laughing
   (78% overlap, delay −0.36s in casey data) + our stitch rule: laughter
   overlays, speech continues UNDER it (laugh_sim mixes, never replaces).
   The rider rule: post-laugh beats AUGMENT, don't pivot.

7. **Articulation economy.** 207 words, none wasted — meaning or rhythm.
   "Like great poetry, comedy is language distilled to its most potent
   form." OUR MAP: verify-match as a craft metric; material slots stay
   tight; the stencil preserves word-level rhythm.
