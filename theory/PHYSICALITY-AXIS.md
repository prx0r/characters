# Physicality axis — does the joke survive closed eyes?

Owner listen-pass finding 2026-10-16: renders were "funny in how bad they
are" for physical comedians. TIC-TALK backs it: kinetic energy r=-0.75
with laughter — the body is a comedy channel we don't render.

| tier | definition | examples | engine lane |
|---|---|---|---|
| voice-first | joke survives audio-only | louis, norm, wright, hedberg, gaffigan, tig, carr, jeselnik, newhart, bargatze, fielder, demetri, pickles-archetype | voice renders land NOW |
| mixed | voice core + physical spikes | ari (stalk-and-spike), pryor-type storytellers | voice works; video amplifies |
| physical-first | body IS the act | casey, kam, will, uncle lazer, carrey, eric andre, kinison, sandler | puppet/lip-sync lane only |

Roster mapping: engines ready for audio show = pickles, norm, louis (+ ari
mixed). casey/kam/will require the video lane (puppet render.py or Fish
Plus lip-sync) — voice-only is a half-act, confirmed by ear + data.

Casting rule: character archetype physicality must match comedian's tier.
Low-physicality characters (Dracula formality, gnome bureaucrat) can wear
voice-first engines. High-physicality characters (gremlins, chaos sprites)
need physical-first engines WITH video.
