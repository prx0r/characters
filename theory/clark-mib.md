## Paraphrased points
- Founding scare: Sept 1953, Albert K. Bender (head of the successful International Flying Saucer Bureau) shuts it down suddenly; tells close associates (esp. Gray Barker) three dark-suited individuals warned him he neared the truth; frightened off the subject. Barker's sensational '"paranoia-drenched" book makes it legend.
- Vintage root: 1905 Welsh religious revival — "man dressed in black" visits a young rural woman three consecutive nights with a message "she is frightened to relate" (Evans, 1905). Same revival produced UFO-like aerial reports.
- Woods (1974, traditions of Satan): the devil "mostly ... is dressed in black, and always in the fashion of the day."
