## Verbatim (short, attributed)
- "Beings sometimes referred to as 'reptoids' or 'reptilians' figure in a number of abduction and contact reports."
- Varieties per one cited source: "the Reptoid (reptilian-humanoid crossbreeds), the various reptilian-gray crossbreed types, and the hierarchical reptilian overlords called the Draco (winged reptilian types)"
- "Draco is a constellation from which, some believe, the reptoids come."
- Carpenter (abduction researcher) on reports from people with no prior exposure: "reporting strikingly similar details in regards [sic] to their anatomy, manner, and behavior" — his gloss: "repulsive and insensitive" reptilian aliens.

## Paraphrased case record
- 17 Nov 1967, Calgary: 13-year-old David Seewaldt crosses a vacant lot, hears high-pitched sound, sees house-sized UFO land. Beam puts him in trance, levitated aboard. Two entities with brown crocodile skin remove his clothes, lead him to an exam room, give him a shot, beam him back. Conscious memory gone; returns 5 months later as vivid dream. Interviewed a year later incl. a university psychologist.
- Mar 1972 Loveland Frogman (Ohio, two policemen) referenced with artist rendition (Fortean Picture Library).
