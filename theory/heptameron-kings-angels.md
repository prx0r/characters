## Day angels (with variants as the file gives them — variants are facts)
- Sun: Michael, with Raphael-attribution variant noted ("Raphael to the Sun and Michael to Mercury" differs from the Concilium reading — file flags it, do not smooth it).
- Moon: Gabriel (GBRI'L).
- Mars: Samael, with Camael variant.
- Mercury: Michael (in the Raphael/Michael swap) else Raphael per other tables — record both, cite the file's own warning.
- Jupiter: Sachiel, with Zadkiel/Satkiel/Saquiel/Sadkiel/Satquiel spellings.
- Venus: Anael, with Hanael variant (Hebrew 'N'L forms in-file).
- Saturn: Cassiel, with Caphriel/Caphciel/Caffiel/Captiel/Zaphkiel cluster.

## Honorius aerial kings (7, from the quoted table — spellings as given)
- Tuesday/Mars: Iammax ("filius dyabuli" gloss in-file — quote-shape, keep verbatim tag).
- Wednesday/Mercury: Habaa. Thursday/Jupiter: Formione (+ Guth). Friday/Venus: Sarabocres (+ Saabotres/Sarabores variants). Saturday/Saturn: Maymon (+ Amaymon variant; Hayton/Assayby/Memmi attendants). Monday: Harthan/Almodab (+ Heletel/Halmital/Hebetel/Almucab/Archam cluster).
- Sunday row partially in the Lucidarium-corrupt zone (Iamax/Guth Mars-Jupiter bleed — file's own caveat; cite, don't clean).
- Winds differ across Honorius vs CLM 849 (file note 19); duplicates flagged in H (note 19b); Ghent 1021A carries sigils for the 7 kings.
