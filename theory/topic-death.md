# DEATH — raw theory notes (topic file)

## Tibetan strand
Source: `/root/projects/source-library/occult/death/sources/tibetan-book-of-dead.txt` (1.1MB; Gyurme Dorje literary history + full translation; translation itself modern-copyrighted — paraphrase, brief quotes only)
- Original title: Bar-do thos-grol chen-mo (Great Liberation by Hearing in the Intermediate States), 12 chapters in the complete English rendering.
- 1927: Lama Kazi Dawa Samdup + W.Y. Evans-Wentz pioneer English rendering of 3 chapters (after-death state + aspirational prayers); Evans-Wentz's title "Tibetan Book of the Dead" retained ever since.
- Transmission: esoteric, restricted inside Tibet until mid-15th century; learned Tibetans noted as surprised at its Western fame.
- Term counts in file: bardo 2 (transliterated Bar-do throughout — search "Bar-do"), forty-nine 3, weighing 3, dharmata 2.
