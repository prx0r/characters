## Governance rule (brief quote, 1583 MS — PD)
King Bobogel, Nov 19: the king and prince govern the whole day, the rest by the six parts of the day — use "with the consideration of the day, their king, their Prince, and his Character," to God's glory. (Paraphrase; pull exact lines from file on stage use.)
