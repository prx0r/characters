## Arrival (verbatim)
> "1145 HOURS- I am making a hasty last entry in the flight log. Several men are approaching on foot toward our aircraft. They are tall with blond hair. In the distance is a large shimmering city pulsating with rainbow hues of color."
> "As we approach, the city seems to be made of a crystal material."
> "One of the beings motions for us to stop before a great door. Over the door is an inscription that I cannot read."
> "I hear now a voice ordering me by name to open the cargo door. I comply."

## Welcome (verbatim)
> "'Have no fear, Admiral, you are to have an audience with the Master...'"
> "'I bid you welcome to our domain, Admiral.' I see a man with delicate features and with the etching of years upon his face."
> "'We have let you enter here because you are of noble character and well-known on the Surface World, Admiral.' Surface World, I half-gasp under my breath! 'Yes,' the Master replies with a smile, 'you are in the domain of the Arianni, the Inner World of the Earth.'"

## Summons reason (verbatim)
> "'Our interest rightly begins just after your race exploded the first atomic bombs over Hiroshima and Nagasaki, Japan. It was at that alarming time we sent our flying machines, the 'Flugelrads,' to your surface world to investigate what your race had done.'"
> "'In 1945 and afterward, we tried to contact your race, but our efforts were met with hostility, our Flugelrads were fired upon.'"

## Storm warning (verbatim)
> "'there is a great storm gathering in your world, a black fury that will not spend itself for many years.'"

## Detention coda (verbatim, from prior pull)
> "I am ORDERED TO REMAIN SILENT IN REGARD TO ALL THAT I HAVE LEARNED, ON THE BEHALF OF HUMANITY!"
> "FOR I HAVE SEEN THAT LAND BEYOND THE POLE, THAT CENTER OF THE GREAT UNKNOWN. ADMIRAL RICHARD E. BYRD United States Navy 24 December 1956"
