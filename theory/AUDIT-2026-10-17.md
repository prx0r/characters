# AUDIT — Standup Simulator Codebase (2026-10-17 23:00)

## The Goal
A standup simulator: user performs a set, gets simulated laughter.
Text in → audio with laughs out.

---

## What Works End-to-End (verified tonight)

### `audition/standup_sim.py` — THE simulator
- **Verified:** produced `audition/audio/kam-sim-test.wav` (4.7MB, 53.3s) at 22:22
- **Pipeline:** text → sentence split → punchline detection (heuristic) → laugh placement (theory rules, no ML) → Fish TTS per beat → ffmpeg concat → laugh clip mixing → WAV
- **Test result:** Kam ep716 transcript → 13 beats, 4 punchlines, 9 laughs, 29% density
- **Bug:** laugh timestamps use estimated durations (words/2.5s), assembly uses actual TTS durations → timing drifts

### `audition/laugh_track_renderer.py` — laugh audio mixer
- **Verified:** loads 6 CC0 clips from `/home/box/comedy-ai/laugh-library/`, mixes at timestamps with ADSR envelopes
- **Problem:** only 4 of 6 clips are usable (2 are applause tracks >10s, filtered out)
- **ADSR:** `laugh_adsr.json` has attack/decay/sustain/release per clip

### `audition/delivery_engine.py` — directed render
- **Verified:** `render_minute(text, comedian)` → beats → mechanisms → pause ladder → Fish TTS with emotion cues → WAV + timeline
- **Test:** Louis Monopoly bit → 19 beats, 73.8s render
- **Note:** module is functions, not a class (docstring lies)

### `audition/timing_profile.py` — text rhythm → delivery
- **Verified:** extracts rhythm features, matches to comedian, outputs delivery params
- **Problem:** I conflated text structure with delivery rhythm. Text tells you WHERE laughs go, delivery tells you WHEN and HOW BIG. They're separate.
