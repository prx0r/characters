## Verbatim (short, attributed)
- "In traditional Japanese lore the Kappa are malicious water demons shaped like monkeys with scales."
- "They lure the unsuspecting into ponds and rivers, then devour them."
- Komatsu Kitamura "theorized that the Kappa were extraterrestrials who came to Japan sometime between the ninth and eleventh centuries" — others add the scaly skin may have been "a spacesuit."

## Paraphrased case record
- Nov 1978, Yokosuka coast: two construction workers fishing see a creature emerge from the sea and glare. Description: ~3 meters tall, thick scaly reptile-like skin, face with two large yellow eyes. Quote-shape: "not a fish, an animal, or a man" (Picasso, 1991).
- 22 Apr 1980, Santa Rosa (Picasso file): motorist sees something fall from sky, car engine dies, cold breeze at foot level, two ~7ft humanoids with webbed hands in black shiny diving suits, skull-like faces, mouths moving without sound. Cold hands around the head, blackout, wakes minutes later half a mile away.
