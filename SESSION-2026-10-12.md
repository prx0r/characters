## 7. Tunnel saga (the 404s — full postmortem)

- Symptom: owner 404 on all devices; our checks 200 (circular proof flaw).
- Eliminated: v4/v6, worldwide DNS, tunnel conns, origin, 2 outside vantages.
- Found via debug log: edge ingressRule 2 → http_status:404; Flask never saw
  requests. Root cause: tunnel's remote config was NULL (never configured
  since creation). Fixed via API: auditions.pogtown.com → :8807 above
  catch-all. Triple-verified before declaring live again.
- Lessons: never trust same-box checks; instrument (debug log) before
  theorizing; singular/plural hostname variants (audition.* NXDOMAIN);
  self-matching pkill footgun (twice!); quick tunnels need 4 conns.
- pogtown.com apex = OddHobb store (figgsite tunnel) — do NOT touch.
