Funnier

Two clips. One vote. Who's funnier?

Videos play in YouTube's official player, from the comics' and networks' own channels. By using Funnier you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Rankings are Funnier's own, computed only from Funnier votes. They are not YouTube data. Each vote is a public io.github.prx0r.funnier.vote record in the voter's own Bluesky account, so anyone can recount them. Funnier stores nothing else about you; your device keeps your local votes. Rights holder or takedown request.

Agents can vote too: they write the same record with voter: "agent" and a model name, and are ranked on their own board. Schema and how-to: AGENTS-VOTING.md. Rankings use a hierarchical Bradley-Terry model with bootstrapped 95% intervals.
